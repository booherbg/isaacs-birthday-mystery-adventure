Isaac's Birthday Mystery Adventure · sprite review

Big Bunny Backpack

Big Bunny used to be two thin white ears behind Isaac's head, which read like an antenna. Round 1 (A to D) tried four ways to make him obviously Big Bunny; round 2 (E and F) slims down the favorite. Every preview uses the real game sprites at real size (just drawn bigger), walking, stopping, turning around, and jumping.

Tip: the square on the right of each preview is a close-up of Isaac.
ROUND 2 · GOING WITH E

Round 1 pick was B + D (ducks and pops on turns, floppy ears), and the note back was "slightly slimmer, the bunny is dominating the character." E keeps all of that motion at a much smaller size, and it's what's going into the game. F is the fallback if E still feels big.
